Write human. Read human.

A Bluesky client that proves a human typed every post.

Speakwrite captures your keystroke dynamics as you type and cryptographically signs each checkpoint with the iPhone’s Secure Enclave. When you publish, the proof goes with the post. When you read, you know it was typed by a person.

How It Works

  1. Sign in Use your Bluesky handle. Face ID unlocks the Secure Enclave signing key.
  2. Type Keystroke dynamics — timing, rhythm, pauses, corrections — are captured natively as you write.
  3. Publish A cryptographic commitment chain is signed by the Secure Enclave and attached to your Bluesky post.
  4. Verify Anyone can check the proof. SHA-256 hash chain + P-256 ECDSA signatures. No server, no trust required.

Why a Native App

A web app can’t do this. Hardware attestation requires a native binary — Apple’s App Attest certifies the app hasn’t been tampered with, and the Secure Enclave holds a signing key that never leaves the silicon. No JavaScript bridge. No web wrapper. The entire proof chain happens inside the signed binary.

What It Proves

It proves

A post was composed through physical typing on a genuine Apple device, authenticated by biometrics, with keystroke behavior consistent with human motor patterns.

It doesn’t prove

That the ideas are original, that no AI was consulted, or that the author didn’t transcribe AI output. It makes deception expensive — not impossible.

Properties

Open protocol
The proof bundle is JSON. Anyone can build a verifier or a compatible client.
Hardware-backed
Secure Enclave + App Attest on every proof. No web fallback.
On-device
No server sees your keystrokes. Only statistical features are committed.
Bluesky native
Full AT Protocol client. Discover feed, verified feed, compose — all in one app.
Progressive trust
One proof is weak. A corpus from the same identity with consistent behavioral patterns is strong.
